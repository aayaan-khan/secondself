Tiago Forte's PARA method organizes digital information into four top-level categories: Projects (short-term efforts in your work or life that you're actively working on with a specific deadline), Areas (long-term responsibilities you want to manage over time like health, finances, or team leadership), Resources (topics or themes of ongoing interest, reference material, research notes, and bookmarks), and Archives (inactive items from the other three categories kept for future reference). The core principle is organizing by actionability rather than topic.
